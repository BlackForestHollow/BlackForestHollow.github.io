Perpetuforest

A procedurally generated survival-horror experience set in a cursed forest where every wrong turn resets the nightmare.

In Development

A forest that learns how you panic.

Perpetuforest is built around disorientation, repetition, and the creeping suspicion that the place itself is responding to your decisions.

  • Procedural routes and pressure points.
  • Survival-horror framing with a strong atmosphere-first identity.
  • A cursed setting built to feel mythic, hostile, and memorable.
Genre
Procedural survival-horror
Focus
Replayability, atmosphere, and pressure-based exploration
